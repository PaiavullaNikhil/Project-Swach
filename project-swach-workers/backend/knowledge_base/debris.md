# 🧱 Standard Operating Procedure: Construction Debris Management

## 1. Overview
Construction debris (concrete slabs, bricks, stone piles, mud piles, iron rods) requires heavy lifting machinery and large vehicles. Standard handcarts are completely inadequate.

## 2. Equipment & PPE Required
* Steel-toed boots
* Heavy leather gloves
* Hard hat (Helmet)
* Safety goggles (for dust protection)
* Shovels, iron bars, and wheelbarrows

## 3. Operations & Safety Protocol
1. **Assess Load**: Verify if the debris is loose (like sand/brick piles) or structured (concrete pillars).
2. **Heavy Lifting Safety**: Lift using legs, not the back. For heavy concrete blocks, use mechanical loaders or request multiple workers.
3. **Iron/Steel Hazards**: Watch out for protruding rusted iron rods. Use wire cutters or hammer them flat before transport.
4. **Dust Control**: If dry sand or cement dust is present, spray water lightly to suppress dust from blowing into neighboring homes.

## 4. Disposal Target
* All building materials must go to the **Municipal Debris Recycling & Aggregates Plant (Sector 11)**.
* Metals should go to the **Industrial Scrap Yard (Sector 8)**.

## 5. Escalation & Priority
* Debris blocking roads: **HIGH PRIORITY** (Needs dump truck and 2+ workers immediately).
* Side-alley debris: Medium priority (within 48 hours).
