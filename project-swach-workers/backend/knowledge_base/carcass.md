# 🐕 Standard Operating Procedure: Animal Carcass Management

## 1. Overview
Dead animal carcasses present immediate biological hazards, foul odors, and severe distress to citizens. This is a highly sensitive category that requires swift, respectful, and hygienic clearance.

## 2. Equipment & PPE Required
* Heavy-duty waterproof rubber gloves
* Protective full-body apron
* Surgical or N95 mask (for odor control and aerosol prevention)
* Bleach spray / Disinfectant solution
* Heavy-duty black leak-proof bags or canvas sheets

## 3. Operations & Safety Protocol
1. **Dignity & Hygiene**: Cover the carcass with a canvas sheet or heavy-duty bag immediately to reduce visibility and odor.
2. **Disinfection**: Spray the ground area thoroughly with bleach solution or sodium hypochlorite after removing the carcass to kill pathogens.
3. **Bagging & Loading**: Carefully lift and place the carcass in double-layered heavy-duty black bags. Seal the bags completely.
4. **Transport Isolation**: Carcasses must be transported in a specialized covered vehicle, completely separated from any other recycling materials.

## 4. Disposal Target
* All carcasses must be taken directly to the **Municipal Veterinary Incinerator (Sector 9)**.
* Under no circumstances should carcasses be dumped in general landfills.

## 5. Escalation & Priority
* Carcass reports: **CRITICAL PRIORITY** (Target clearance time: under 3 hours).
* The Dispatcher must prioritize specialized workers with animal-control training and closed vans.
