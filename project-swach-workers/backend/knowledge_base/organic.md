# 🍂 Standard Operating Procedure: Organic & Wet Waste Management

## 1. Overview
Organic waste includes kitchen waste, food scraps, fallen leaves, tree branches, and flower market waste. If left uncollected, it rots, attracts pests, and creates public health issues.

## 2. Equipment & PPE Required
* Standard latex/rubber gloves
* Reflective vest
* Broom and leaf rakes
* Biodegradable green collection bags (Green color-coded)

## 3. Operations & Safety Protocol
1. **Sweep & Gather**: Use leaf rakes to pile up fallen leaves and branches. Gather organic kitchen piles into a pile.
2. **Moisture Isolation**: Ensure wet food scraps are packed inside leak-proof compostable bags to prevent liquid dripping during transport.
3. **Pest Safety**: Be cautious of stray animals, rodents, or bees nested in large organic piles or compost heaps.

## 4. Disposal Target
* Wet waste and kitchen scraps must go directly to the **Sector 5 Composting and Bio-Gas Plant** to generate green energy and fertilizer.
* Garden waste/dry branches should go to the **Sector 3 Leaf Shredding Yard**.

## 5. Escalation & Priority
* Leaf piles: Low priority (within 36 hours).
* Overflowing hotel/market organic waste: **HIGH PRIORITY** (must be cleared same-day).
