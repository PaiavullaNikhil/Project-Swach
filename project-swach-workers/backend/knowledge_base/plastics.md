# ♻️ Standard Operating Procedure: Plastic Waste Management

## 1. Overview
Plastic waste represents one of the most common municipal litter problems. Effective management requires prompt collection and proper routing to approved recycling hubs to prevent environmental contamination and drain clogging.

## 2. Equipment & PPE Required
* Heavy-duty gloves
* High-visibility reflective vest
* Standard collection cart or light utility vehicle (LUV)
* Recyclable waste bags (Blue color-coded)

## 3. Operations & Safety Protocol
1. **Identification**: Identify the plastic types (PET bottles, bags, multi-layered packaging, hard plastics).
2. **Sorting on Site**: Separate large plastics from organic mud or stones if possible to reduce contamination.
3. **Bagging**: Pack the collected plastics into designated Blue recycling bags. Secure the bags with double ties.
4. **Transport**: Load the bags onto a utility cart or truck. Ensure no loose plastics fly off during transport.

## 4. Disposal Target
* All sorted and clean plastics must be routed strictly to the **Namma Metro Composting & Recycling Hub (Sector 4)**.
* Low-grade plastics (wrappers, thin bags) must be routed to the **Municipal Waste-to-Energy Plant (Sector 12)** for pyrolysis/fuel processing.

## 5. Escalation & Priority
* Standard plastic litter: Low priority (resolved within 24 hours).
* Blocked storm drains due to plastic: High priority (immediate dispatch of a clearance team).
